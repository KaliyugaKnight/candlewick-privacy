Privacy Policy for CandleWick

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

CandleWick runs locally on your device and does not collect, store, or share personal data.

Microphone Access

When you enable blow-to-extinguish, microphone sound is checked locally in real time only to detect blowing on the flame. No audio is recorded, saved, or transmitted off your device.
